Privacy

Effective 20 October 2026

modelspec.org is a static documentation site with a registry of published models. It has no accounts, no advertising and does not sell data. This page says what happens when you visit it, and the one choice you control: analytics.

Fonts from Google

To display the site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) on every page, before and regardless of your analytics choice. For these loads Google receives your IP address and browser details. They set no analytics cookies and do not depend on your analytics choice.

Registry search

On the Registry page, text you type into the search field is sent to search.openvaultdb.com, the registry search service run for OpenVaultDB. It is not a Google host, and it does not depend on your analytics choice.

Analytics

Google Analytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited, runs on modelspec.org only if you choose “Accept analytics” in the analytics banner. Until you accept, no analytics script loads and no analytics cookie is set.

After you accept, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_<id>, kept for up to two years) and receives the page address and the referring page without their query or fragment, browser and device information, and an approximate location that Google derives from your IP address. Google Signals and advertising features are switched off.

The legal basis is your consent. We keep Google Analytics event data for no longer than 14 months.

Your choice and withdrawing it

Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage, key modelspec.analytics-consent.v1). You can change it at any time with “Analytics choices” in the site footer or the button below. Choosing “Reject” stops analytics and deletes the _ga cookies on this site.
